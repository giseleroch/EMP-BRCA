# In Silico and Immunohistochemical Identification of Epithelial-Mesenchymal Plasticity and Stemness Biomarkers in Breast Cancer
This project investigates prognostic biomarkers associated with epithelial–mesenchymal plasticity and tumor stem cell biology in breast cancer. Integrating network and survival analyses of TCGA Firehose Legacy data with immunohistochemical assessment in a clinical cohort, the study identified subtype-specific candidate markers: CCNB1 and CDCA8 in triple-negative tumors, ECM2 and P4HA3 in HER2-positive tumors, and FOSB and LEP in hormone receptor-positive tumors. These findings support further investigation of their potential for prognostic stratification.
